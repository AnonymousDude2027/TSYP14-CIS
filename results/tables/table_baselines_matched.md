# Table baselines-c - the proposed suite at three operating points

The same suite, re-thresholded from its own calibration table at budgets 0.1, 0.5 and 1.0 alarms per sensor-year, on the baseline injection set (step and ramp, 1-3 sigma). It exists so that the comparison with a baseline can be made at a *matched* false-alarm rate rather than at a matched nominal budget.
